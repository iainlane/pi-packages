A verdict now applies only when the session still matches what the review read. When the person's messages or answers change during a review, for example when they add a restriction, the reviewer reviews again with the new evidence, up to three attempts within the review's deadline. When the action, the registered tools, the context edits, the session or the branch changes, the ask defers. Moving through the session tree, compaction, switching sessions and forking cancel reviews in progress.
